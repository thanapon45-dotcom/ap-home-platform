## Cross-page finding worth acting on (live-verified, now 8-page sample)

Across the 8 pages actually checked via live browser access, engagement did not track follower count. SpyEstates (~210K followers, news/investigation-style content) had the strongest engagement of any page (21 reactions, 8 comments, 3 shares). Bahn Mahanakorn (~110K followers, home-building, behind-the-scenes video) came second (9 reactions, 2 shares, 164 views). HOME888 (~120K followers, geo-focused round, amenity-proximity listing video) got only 3 reactions + 1,700 views. Bangkok Asset (~350K followers, direct project-promo) got only 5 reactions + 448 views despite the largest follower count of any page checked. The smallest pages (Mahanakornhome, CS Property, Home.ban2 — all plain listing formats) scored lowest across the board, mostly 0-3 reactions and no comments/shares.

This is now an 8-post comparison across 8 pages, not a statistically validated pattern — it should be treated as a real, worth-testing signal, not a proven rule. But it's consistent enough across a growing sample, now spanning both the original Bangkok-wide round and the Nonthaburi/Pathum Thani geo-focused round, to support one specific, evidence-backed recommendation: a "news/announcement", "investigative", or "behind-the-scenes/trust-building" framing appears to meaningfully outperform plain price-listing posts, regardless of a page's follower count.

## Two major findings from this round of live verification

1. **Bangkok Asset and Bangkok Asset Center are the same company.** A memorial post on Bangkok Asset Center's page was explicitly attributed to "บริษัท บางกอก แอสเซท อินเตอร์กรุ๊ป จำกัด (มหาชน)" (Bangkok Asset Intergroup PUBLIC Company Limited), and both pages share an identical registered address. This resolves the disambiguation flagged in the previous version of this report: they are not unrelated competitors, and not a duplicate/typo — they are two Facebook Pages run by the same public company, apparently splitting functions (Bangkok Asset = resale marketing/premium listings; Bangkok Asset Center = "we buy your house" cash-acquisition service, and much less actively posted-to).

2. **Bahn Mahanakorn (บ้านมหานคร รับสร้างบ้าน) is a custom home-building company, not a resale page**, despite its name's similarity to "Mahanakornhome" (a small resale page also in this database — confirmed as two distinct, unrelated entities). At ~110K followers, Facebook-verified, and actively posting, this is the most strategically relevant competitor found so far, since it is the only one confirmed to share Finnhouses' actual core business (building new custom homes) rather than buying/reselling/renovating existing ones.

## National media/platform pages checked — mostly lower direct relevance

FazWaz (~36K followers, English-language, international investor audience), CBRE Thailand (~61K, commercial real estate B2B), CheckRaka (~160K, general price-comparison media, not real-estate-exclusive), and REIC (~23K, official government market-data body) were all live-verified this round. None of them compete directly with Finnhouses on its actual audience (local Thai homebuyers/sellers in Finnhouses' service areas) or business model (home building/resale) — they serve international investors, corporate clients, general consumers, or government statistics respectively. They're useful as market-context sources (especially REIC for official data and CBRE for market commentary) but should not be treated as head-to-head competitors in the way Bangkok Asset, SpyEstates, or especially Bahn Mahanakorn are.
