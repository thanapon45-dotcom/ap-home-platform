# Investment Decision Support

**Coverage: 60% — Partial.** ROI, land, and deal data all exist individually
— what's missing is a view that helps decide *between* opportunities, not
just evaluate one at a time.

## What exists

- ROI Analysis, Land Analysis, and Fix & Flip Analysis (see respective docs)
  each support single-deal decision-making

## What's missing

- No portfolio-level comparison (ranking multiple prospective deals against
  each other)
- No capital allocation view across active + prospective deals
