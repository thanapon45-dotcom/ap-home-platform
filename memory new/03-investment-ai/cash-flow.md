# Cash Flow

**Coverage: 30% — Partial, minimal.** Given this department carries 60% of
company revenue, this is arguably the single highest-priority gap on the
entire platform.

## What exists

- `Deals.tsx` — a single "capital deployed" metric per deal

## What's missing

- No dedicated cash flow tracker
- No forward-looking cash position across active/prospective deals
- No timing view (when capital goes out vs. when it's expected back from a
  sale)

## Why this matters more than it looks

Fix & Flip is a capital-intensive, timing-sensitive business — a platform
that tracks ROI well (see [roi-analysis.md](./roi-analysis.md)) but not cash
timing can show a deal is profitable on paper while missing whether the
company can actually fund the next acquisition while capital is tied up in
the current one.
