# ADR-027 — Separate Build-to-Sell and Renovate-to-Resell investment streams

**Status:** Accepted / Production  
**Date:** 2026-09-21

## Decision

Land Analyzer and Fix & Flip Deals represent different real-estate investment models and must remain separate in the data model.

- **Land Analyzer → projects** = **Build-to-Sell**: evaluate land and development economics for building houses to sell.
- **Fix & Flip Deals → reno_deals** = **Renovate-to-Resell**: acquire an existing property, renovate it, and resell it.

There is no identity or foreign-key relationship between projects and reno_deals.

Operational Fix & Flip work may connect:

```text
reno_deals.site_id → sites.id
qc_inspections.deal_id → reno_deals.id
qc_inspections.site_id → sites.id
```

Cross-module context is available through Brains, but Brains must not infer a Land Project ↔ Deal identity relationship.

## Production verification

On 2026-09-21 the production schema was checked after cleanup:

- reno_deals.land_project_id does not exist.
- reno_deals_land_project_id_fkey does not exist.
- reno_deals.site_id remains.
- qc_inspections.deal_id and qc_inspections.site_id remain.

The previous ADR-022 Land Analyzer → Fix & Flip coupling is superseded and retained only as historical decision history.
